# Account Diagnosis

## Diagnostic dimensions

### 1. Identity clarity

Check whether a first-time viewer can answer within seconds:

- 这是谁？
- 做什么？
- 为什么值得继续看？
- 可能能帮我什么？

### 2. Audience fit

Check whether recent content appears to serve one coherent audience. Do not demand one narrow audience if the business legitimately serves several groups; instead create clearly separated content pillars.

### 3. Content pillars

Classify recent posts into:

- 认知/流量
- 专业/决策
- 人格/IP
- 案例/证据
- 场景/工作
- 产品/服务
- 转化

Look for over-concentration and missing trust-building content.

### 4. Speaker consistency

For fixed-person accounts, compare topic and language with the persona profile.

For rotating-person accounts, assess whether the account has a stable promise despite speaker changes.

### 5. Conversion path

Map:

视频 → 主页 → 私信/评论 → 咨询/报价 → 到店/合作/成交

If a step is missing, do not simply add a hard sales CTA. First diagnose whether trust or product understanding is missing.

### 6. Content execution

Check whether the account's ideas are actually filmable with the user's resources.

## Output

Use this structure:

**现状** → **问题** → **证据** → **优先处理** → **下一轮测试**

Avoid labels such as“垃圾账号”“废号”“算法限流号” unless an authoritative source or concrete evidence supports them.
