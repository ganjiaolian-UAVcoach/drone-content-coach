# Topic Engine

## Inputs

A topic can come from:

- viewer questions;
- search terms;
- comments;
- current industry events;
- policy/regulation changes;
- product releases;
- real work incidents;
- training mistakes;
- seasonal agricultural work;
- customer objections;
- common misconceptions;
- the user's own idea.

## Topic transformation

Do not stop at the raw keyword.

Transform:

**signal → audience question → role-specific angle → usable content promise**

Example signal:

> “无人机配送又上热点”

Possible role-specific angles:

- 培训机构：以后想做配送方向，学习和岗位到底看什么能力？
- 教练：培训里哪些基础能力会真正影响后续实操？
- 飞手：配送场景与普通作业在工作方式上有哪些不同？
- 厂家：为什么不同任务对载荷、续航和环境适应能力要求不同？

## Topic quality gate

Prefer a topic when it has at least two of:

- real user need;
- first-hand expertise;
- visual demonstration;
- timely relevance;
- decision value;
- strong speaker fit.

## Anti-copy rule

Do not copy the original creator's framing just because it performed well. Rebuild the topic using the user's own work experience, evidence and point of view.

## Trend caution

A trend is a source of attention, not a permission slip to make unsupported claims. For current events, confirm the factual basis first.
