# Persona System

## The five-layer persona card

Every recurring speaker should have five layers:

### 1. Role

Who is this person professionally?

Examples:

- 机构老板
- 无人机教练
- 吊运飞手
- 飞防飞手
- 产品经理
- 工程师
- 售后负责人

### 2. Personality

Use practical content traits rather than psychological diagnoses:

- 直接 / 委婉
- 冷静 / 热情
- 严谨 / 随和
- 实战 / 理论
- 幽默 / 克制
- 爱讲故事 / 爱讲结论

### 3. Language fingerprint

Track:

- common opening phrases;
- sentence length;
- use of slang;
- use of industry terms;
- preferred analogies;
- whether the person likes questions/rhetorical questions;
- common endings.

Never fabricate a catchphrase. Record it only after the user provides examples or approves generated phrasing.

### 4. Camera behavior

Record:

- comfortable distance;
- whether eye contact is natural;
- whether hands move a lot;
- whether demonstrations are stronger than talking;
- suitable shot size;
- tolerance for retakes.

### 5. Topic fit and boundaries

Record:

- topics the person can explain from real experience;
- topics requiring company approval;
- topics the person should not speak for the company on;
- topics that require official-source verification.

## Fixed vs rotating accounts

For a rotating team account, maintain two levels:

**Account voice:** stable content promise, audience, worldview and conversion goal.

**Speaker voice:** each person's role, personality and expertise.

Do not flatten multiple speakers into a generic “brand voice”.

## Speaker-topic matching

Before writing a script, score conceptually (not numerically in the user-facing answer unless useful):

- professional authority;
- first-hand experience;
- camera suitability;
- business relevance;
- audience trust.

The speaker with the strongest fit becomes the recommended speaker.
