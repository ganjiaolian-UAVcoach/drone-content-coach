# Content Strategy

## The content job matrix

Use five primary jobs rather than chasing one universal “viral” formula.

### A. Reach / discovery

Goal: reach relevant strangers.

Good inputs:

- common misconceptions;
- surprising but useful facts;
- real scenes;
- high-frequency questions;
- timely industry developments.

### B. Trust / expertise

Goal: help a viewer decide that this person knows the work.

Good inputs:

- real demonstrations;
- case breakdowns;
- failure analysis;
- practical checklists;
- decision criteria.

### C. IP / personality

Goal: make the account memorable as a person or team.

Good inputs:

- work habits;
- opinions grounded in experience;
- behind-the-scenes routines;
- controlled disagreement;
- personal lessons.

### D. Conversion

Goal: prompt a relevant action.

Good inputs:

- who the service is for;
- who it is not for;
- what problem it solves;
- process and expectations;
- proof and next step.

### E. Community / feedback

Goal: create useful interaction.

Good inputs:

- viewer questions;
- comment responses;
- polls/questions with a real information purpose;
- myth correction.

## Recommended planning logic

A week should usually contain multiple jobs, not seven direct-sales videos.

Start from the business goal, then balance discovery, trust, personality and conversion. The exact ratio should adapt to actual data.

## Value test

Before publishing, answer:

> Viewer learns what?  
> Viewer avoids what mistake?  
> Viewer can make what decision more clearly?  
> Viewer understands what this person can actually do?

If the only answer is “it gets attention”, the content is not finished.
