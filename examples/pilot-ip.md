# Example: Heavy-Lift Pilot

Role: 吊运飞手  
Account: 个人号  
On-camera: 真人边工作边讲  
Goal: 找项目 / 找合作 / 招徒

## Topic

“吊运飞手真正难的，不一定是把东西吊起来。”

## Content value

Show a real project decision:

- why the site was checked;
- what variables affect the lift;
- where the risk comes from;
- what the operator confirms before execution.

Avoid publishing sensitive client information or presenting unsafe procedures as entertainment.
