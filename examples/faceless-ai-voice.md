# Example: Faceless + AI Voice

Role: 低空行业知识账号  
On-camera: 无真人，真实画面 + AI配音  
Goal: 行业认知 + 精准搜索流量

## Rule

No face does not mean no personality.

The account still needs:

- a stable editorial worldview;
- consistent vocabulary;
- recurring structure;
- real footage;
- evidence and source discipline.

## Avoid

Do not become a machine that reads public news word-for-word over generic drone footage.

## Better

Start from a real industry question, explain the context, show relevant footage, make a useful distinction, and state what remains uncertain.
